Help with KeepSake

Your memories, on your device.

No online account. No advertising or tracking SDKs. KeepSake stores your library locally, with sharing and exported backups under your control.

Keep a copy safe.

Save a library backup outside the app. Reinstalling KeepSake alone does not restore stories lost with an app or device.
